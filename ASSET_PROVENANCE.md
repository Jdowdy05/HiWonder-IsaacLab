# Asset provenance and rights

This is an owner-authorized copy of the custom HiWonder Isaac Lab project. Its software and CAD-derived assets have distinct provenance.

The extension's existing Python source headers declare BSD-3-Clause and are preserved unchanged. `source/isaaclab_hiwonder/pyproject.toml` declares the same software license. Root `LICENSE` preserves the upstream notice/disclaimer for the Isaac Lab-derived software and integration manifest. The small repository setup helper and its tests are provided under BSD-3-Clause as well. Dependencies such as Isaac Sim, PyTorch, Newton, RSL-RL and optional tools retain their own licenses and acceptance requirements.

The `stock` HiWonder follower USDs and scale variants derive from owner-supplied assembled follower CAD. Their source hash, individual source object/link assignments, camera assumptions, conversion approximations and output hashes are recorded in `source/isaaclab_hiwonder/isaaclab_hiwonder/assets/generated/manifest.json`. Original stock CAD input bytes are not included. The stock geometry remains available and selected by default. Absolute paths embedded as historical provenance are not runtime installation requirements.

The external regeneration input is `follower arm black.3dm`, 176,591,534 bytes, SHA-256 `f675f6049f94206c7b74ab73c40485913916babff1a6f4fc145dc9040672a2f8`. It exceeds GitHub's normal file limit and is intentionally omitted; no Git LFS dependency is required to use the included runtime assets. Keep the original input externally if CAD regeneration is needed.

The separate `eka_v6` variant uses the owner-supplied `Eka_XL330_SO100_V6_Distal_Assembly_Printable_Parts.step`, preserved unchanged as `source/isaaclab_hiwonder/isaaclab_hiwonder/assets/eka_v6/source.step`. Its SHA-256 is `141ab8a721d850ed88da9d97f17257ebce1f85cfca7a0403f4c6e20c1f60e944`. The Eka manifest records source ownership, per-scale USD outputs, mounting transform, estimated inertials and qualification limits. The original supplied STEP is distinct from the repaired derivative and was not overwritten.

The derived printed geometry uses exact NURBS reparameterization for 13 parts. Two caps required user-authorized boundary patches; their derived CAD remains faceted and does not recover the original parametric feature history. Repair records identify patched boundaries and retained contact triangles. At actual USD float precision, the printable meshes were checked for watertightness, consistent winding and positive volume across nominal, 0.99 and 1.01 scale. These checks concern digital geometry, not physical printing, fastener fit or simulator contact acceptance.

Reference hardware/case meshes come from the earlier native V6 assembly. That reference source does not supply the changed printed parts, which come from the new STEP. Mounting uses measured corresponding four-hole patterns on a 14 mm pitch circle, after `wrist_roll`. Screw/thread engagement, physical clearances, joint limits, masses, inertias and XL330 dynamic parameters remain unmeasured or estimated.

CAD licensing and permission for public redistribution of the original model or its derivatives have **not** been established. A software BSD declaration does not grant rights to these CAD assets. Public release or redistribution requires separate rights confirmation.

`stable_peg_grasp.json` and `stable_peg_grasp.npz` are retained because SGS loads the qualified **stock-only** simulated reset state. They are small runtime assets, not trained policy weights. Any local evidence path inside their metadata records the historical experiment; the packaged loader uses the bundled files. Eka needs a new asset-specific grasp receipt; the stock seed cannot qualify its four independent finger joints.

Eka has nine actions and ten rigid bodies: five arm joints plus two finger chains with independently actuated root/tip joints. Its actor has 27 proprioceptive values and its critic capacity is 320, versus stock's six actions, seven bodies, 18 proprioceptive values and capacity 256. Old stock checkpoints are incompatible with the Eka dimensions. Keeping stock as the default does not guarantee exact checkpoint/bank replay across changed implementation fingerprints.

Both variants retain 640x480 wrist capture at a configured 30 Hz. Eka retains the original wrist-relative camera pose and optical assumptions, but the new physical attachment is provisional. Independent mount measurement, lens intrinsics/distortion calibration and held-out validation remain necessary. Historical all-30 GPU/contact/training receipts concern stock; the Eka CPU contracts do not extend those receipts to the new hand. The assets establish simulation geometry/configuration, not physical acceptance.
